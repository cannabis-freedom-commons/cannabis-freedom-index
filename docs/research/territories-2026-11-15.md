## French departments (gp, mq, gf): French national law, as countries/re.yaml cites (csp, cidj, sp_use, sp, cx, onisr)
- Guadeloupe — OFDT note, June 2026 (https://www.ofdt.fr/publication/2026/drogues-et-conduites-addictives-en-guadeloupe-2691):
  herb grown locally or imported from neighbouring islands, the Dominican Republic most cited; herb
  the most accessible illegal product; lowest price 4 €/g (20 € for 5 g), about half the mainland
  price, joints from 2 €; resin rarer, mainly from mainland France (Moroccan origin), sometimes
  bartered; strains "skunk", "creepy", "bata skunk"; 2021 adult use in the year lower than mainland.
- Martinique — OFDT note, August 2024, 2022 data (https://www.ofdt.fr/publication/2024/pratiques-d-usages-et-prise-en-charge-des-usagers-de-drogues-en-martinique-en-2022):
  herb market very dynamic, seized herb mostly from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, local production
  too; no local resin, resin from Morocco via mainland France, bartered 1–2.5 kg resin per kg of
  cocaine (2021); 10 € buys 6–8 g of herb or 1 g of resin (Paris 2022: 7–10 €/g herb).
- French Guiana — OFDT OUDOM report, July 2025 (https://www.ofdt.fr/publication/2025/drogues-et-conduites-addictives-en-guyane-en-2024-2549):
  cannabis mostly herb, widely available from local production and imports; resin market growing,
  from Morocco via Europe, exchanged for cocaine; Cayenne airport controls reinforced from 2022
  (mules carry cocaine); 2014 regular cannabis use 3.8%.

## Cook Islands
- Narcotics and Misuse of Drugs Act 2004 (PacLII refused automated reads; read by hand):
  possession up to 2 years / $5,000 (as High Court sentencing notes describe); cultivation up to
  20 years; utensils up to 5 years / $5,000. 2009 amendment reportedly allows regulations licensing
  medicinal cultivation (Cook Islands News letter, 15 May 2023, https://cookislandsnews.com/?p=257084);
  none enacted then. 30+ plants seized in Arorangi, October 2021 (https://cookislandsnews.com/?p=232673).

## Turks and Caicos
- Control of Drugs Ordinance 1976, revised laws 03.14 (https://gov.tc/agc/2021-revised-laws);
  cannabis Class B; possession 3 years/$40,000 summary, 7 years on indictment; intent to supply
  4 years/$50,000 summary, 15 years on indictment (Wikipedia, citing the ordinance; verify in text).
  No medical or decriminalisation law found.

## New Caledonia (paka)
- Deliberation 562 of 5 June 2026 on the hemp sector strategic plan (juridoc.gouv.nc), text extracted.
- CBD listed as a narcotic like THC; January 2024 Congress motions to remove it (LNC, https://www.lnc.nc/print/1423451).
- Camp-Est smuggling case: 536 g, two years' prison, October 2026 (https://www.lnc.nc/print/1432390).

## Western Sahara
- Morocco holds about 70% (all cities, coast), Polisario about 30% (Wikipedia). Moroccan 1974 law
  (cite as in countries/ma.yaml). 12 t resin incinerated in Laayoune, 1 July 2020; about 6 t
  seized at Oum Dreyga, 13 February 2022 (Morocco World News). No source on Polisario-zone law.
